Order notice

Doughnuts, cookies & chips: order 24 hrs ahead. Custom & celebration cakes: 3 to 5 days.

Delivery across Tamale. The fee depends on your area and is confirmed on WhatsApp with your order total.

Pickup

Free pickup in Tamale at an agreed time. The exact location is shared when your order is confirmed.

Payment

Cash on delivery or pickup, or full payment upfront via Mobile Money. See payment options below.

Payment

Two simple ways to pay

Choose what suits you when you place your order on WhatsApp.

Option 1

Cash on Delivery or Pickup

Pay in cash when your order arrives or when you collect it. No payment needed before we bake.

  • Pay the rider on delivery, or pay at pickup
  • Please have the exact amount ready
  • Delivery fee confirmed on WhatsApp
Option 2

Full Payment Upfront

Pay the full amount in advance through Mobile Money (MoMo). Your order is confirmed as soon as payment is received.

  1. Send your order on WhatsApp
  2. We reply with your total and our MoMo details
  3. Send the payment and share the screenshot or reference
  4. We confirm and start baking

Our story

A recipe is never just a recipe

Mystery Bakebite started in a home kitchen in Tamale with one simple belief: every recipe has something unique waiting to be discovered. The "mystery" is that little twist: the extra cream in a milky donut, the flavour pairing you didn't expect, that turns a good bake into your favourite one.

Everything is still made by hand, in small batches, by Emmanuella N. Awini herself. No factory, no shortcuts, just good ingredients and a lot of love. Alongside the bakes, she teaches classes and creates custom pieces for Tamale's celebrations.
